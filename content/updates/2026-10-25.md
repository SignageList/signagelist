Seven products joined the directory, all suggested by the community.

Three are conventional cloud CMSs at different ends of the market. **Signital** runs as a native app on the panel itself, on Samsung Tizen, LG webOS and Android displays, so a network can skip media players entirely; it is billed only for displays that were online that month. **SignageStack** sells one plan with everything in it at EUR 11 per screen. **GlücksCast** comes from a Nuremberg IT shop and is the only one of the three with a self-hosted licence alongside the hosted plans.

Two are built for a single trade. **DopeCast** does cannabis dispensary menu boards, with direct POS integrations that pull prices and stock every few minutes, and **CedMenu** designs restaurant menu boards to order and ships them on Android player boxes.

The last two are priced unlike anything else in the directory. **Better Signage** charges for storage instead of screens, so every plan from the free 50 MB tier upwards carries unlimited Apple TVs. **RedSquare** charges nothing at all: venues install a free player, approve what plays, and set a price per 10-second slot, and the platform takes a cut of each booking instead of a subscription.
