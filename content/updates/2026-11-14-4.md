## MangoApps

**MangoApps** is an employee platform for frontline and office teams, and digital signage is one of its apps. Any screen with a browser becomes a workplace display, fed from the same place staff get news and announcements. It isn't sold on its own: suites are priced per employee, from $4 a month with a 200-employee minimum, and team suites come with a 14-day trial.

It also has one of the longer compliance lists in the directory: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HITRUST, HIPAA with a Business Associate Agreement, and GDPR with a Data Processing Agreement, all stated on its own security page.

## HITRUST and TX-RAMP

Two standards are now tracked. [HITRUST](/compliance/hitrust/) is the certified healthcare security framework many US hospital systems ask vendors for. Uniguest holds it for its pCare patient system, alongside MangoApps. [TX-RAMP](/compliance/tx-ramp/) is the certification Texas state agencies and public universities need before buying a cloud service. Carousel and OptiSigns both hold Level 1.

## Platform fixes

Eight products had incomplete platform lists. CedMenu, Menuboard Manager, Pixage and RedSquare now show Fire OS. DSMenu, SignStream, Taplist and Untappd for Business already listed Fire OS and Amazon Signage, and now show Android too.
