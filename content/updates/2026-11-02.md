Product pages now carry a "Reviews elsewhere" panel linking to the product's page on third-party review sites, with the rating and review count as read on the day.

84 of the 641 listings have one, all from G2, matched against G2's own digital signage category. The rating is a dated snapshot rather than a live figure, so each panel says when it was read and points at the source for the current number. The same information appears in each product's Markdown twin, so the machine-readable copies do not drift from the page.

Two sources that were on the list are not here. Capterra sits behind a human-verification challenge, and Gartner Peer Insights runs a digital signage market of 30 products but does not expose stable per-product links in a way that can be matched reliably. Both would need a different route, most likely an official one.

The schema now has a `reviews` block with room for all three, so adding the others later is a data change rather than a rebuild.
