The GDPR is the EU law on personal data. Like HIPAA, it has no official certification, so a vendor's claim means it says it follows the regulation, usually backed by a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and a list of subprocessors.

For signage, personal data includes user accounts in the CMS, and anything collected at the screen: camera analytics, audience measurement, visitor check-in or forms. Where the vendor hosts data matters too, so check whether EU hosting is offered.
