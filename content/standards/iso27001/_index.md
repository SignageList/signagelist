ISO/IEC 27001 certifies that a vendor runs a managed information security program: risk assessments, documented controls, and yearly audits by an accredited certification body. It is the standard European and Asian buyers ask for most, much as US buyers ask for SOC 2.

Check the certificate's scope. A certificate that covers only a head office or a hardware factory says little about the cloud service that runs your screens.
