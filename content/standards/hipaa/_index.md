HIPAA is the US law that protects patient health information. There is no official HIPAA certification, so a vendor's claim means it says its service meets HIPAA's security and privacy rules, and often that it will sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA).

A BAA matters when screens show patient data, such as waiting-room queues with names, room boards or staff dashboards. If your screens only show general content, you may not need one. Each listing links to the vendor's own statement.
