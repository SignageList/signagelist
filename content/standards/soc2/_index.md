SOC 2 is an audit, run by an independent CPA firm, of how a vendor protects customer data. A Type I report checks that the controls exist on one date; a Type II report checks that they worked over a period, usually 6 to 12 months, so Type II carries more weight.

For signage, the audit covers the vendor's cloud CMS and the people who run it: who can reach your screens, how changes are reviewed and how incidents are handled. Vendors share the report under NDA, so ask for it during procurement.
